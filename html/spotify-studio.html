Making Music Production Familiar and Simple.

Objective

Making Music Simple

People love music. But when it comes to music production, it can feel like a world only a few can fully step into.

Research

Understanding the barrier to music production

To better understand our target audience, we conducted an anonymous survey of over 50 people, gathering insights into their interests and preferences.

Insights

What do Spotify users want?

01

Most display interest in listening to cover/remix versions

02

Many were interested in creating covers

03

Most would prefer to edit on desktop

04

Many cited lack of knowledge as a barrier

Problem Statement

In order to lower the barrier to entry and make music production more accessible to a wider audience, we propose to combine the familiarity of Spotify's interface with an intuitive and simplified sound editor.

Solution

Introducing Spotify Studio

A simple sound editor featured as a extension on the Spotify app, allowing users to pick songs to remix or share their own song covers.

Solution

Familiar UI

The user experience starts right from the homepage - where everything is styles as close as possible to the original Spotify app. This encourages users to explore the new feature, while still feeling secure and in control.

Solution

Add instrumentals without leaving the flow

The Studio is designed minimally, with the tracks of the selected song added. A + button allows users to add new tracks such as instrumentals and vocals.

Solution

Experiment with Effects

The kebab button on tracks allows users to adjust the selected track to personalize the song.

Solution

Add lyrics to Sing with Confidence

Users can use the same button on Spotify's normal home bar to pull up a Karaoke panel that will track the lyrics and place it with the song.

Solution

Save and Personalize your project

Users can save their project when they are done, changing the cover, name, and viewing availability similarly to saving a playlist.

Key Decisions

Making Choices Simple

Keeping in mind our target demographic of beginners we limited the steps it takes to start a project, as well as giving users inspiration for their project through a explore section.

Key Decisions

Making Song of Origin Clear

If a user has published their cover or remix, it will appear in their homepage. A extra label has been added to clarify they are not the original owner.
