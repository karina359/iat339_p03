Interactive Elements

In-text link

CSS selector:

.in-text

Sample code:

< a href="product.html">product page</a>

Rendered element:

Visit our Menu Page to explore our selection of drinks and cakes that we have to offer.

Navigation Link

CSS selector:

.button-nav

Sample code:

<a href="product.html" class="button-nav">Products</a>

Rendered element:

Products

Image as a Link

CSS selector:

.img-container, .image, .overlay, .text

Sample code:

<div class="img-container">

<img class="image" src="../img copy/location.png" alt="Inside of teatime cafe">

</div>
</a>
</div>

Text Elements

Headings

CSS selector:

h1, h2, h3, h4

Sample code:

<h1>Heading 1</h1> <h2>Heading 2</h2> <h3>Heading 3</h3> <h4>Heading 4</h4>

Heading 1

Heading 2

Heading 3

Paragraphs

CSS selector:

p

Sample code:

<p>TeaTime is a modern cafe based in Vancouver. We serve a variety of items ranging from coffee, teas, and cakes. We welcome everyone in our cafe!.</p>

Rendered element:

TeaTime is a modern cafe based in Vancouver. We serve a variety of items ranging from coffee, teas, and cakes. We welcome everyone in our cafe!

Numbered Lists

CSS selector:

ol

Sample code:

<ol> <li>Choose your drink</li> <li>Choose a cake</li> <li>Place your order!</li> </ol>

Rendered element:

  1. Choose your drink
  2. Choose a cake
  3. Place your order!
